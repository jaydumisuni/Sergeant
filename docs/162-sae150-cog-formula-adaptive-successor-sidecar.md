## 4. Three outputs from every real improvement

A successful product generation may create three distinct outputs:

1. **Product improvement** — the product itself improved.
2. **Reusable lesson candidate** — another product may benefit from the principle.
3. **Cognitive improvement candidate** — COG/Formula/SRG/Tenfold may gain a better way to reason about related worlds.

These three outputs have separate authority and qualification.

A product improvement does not automatically prove either reusable intelligence output.
