## 16. Executor freedom and prohibition

Allowed executor behavior includes parallel fixture preparation, Python/Rust construction, hostile tests, donor study, independent capability development, speculative downstream work isolated from unresolved upstream truth and dynamic reallocation over the dependency frontier.

Forbidden executor behavior includes removing a programme obligation, changing proof dependencies, weakening qualification, reinterpreting UNKNOWN, changing RAB/ACR authority, declaring a programme PROVEN from microtask completion, activating partial generations, retrofitting PR #167, or changing roadmap semantics without a new owner-approved roadmap generation.

## 17. Success and reopen law

The campaign is complete only when `SAE-180 = PROVEN` and all transitive founding obligations close. Anything less is implementation/qualification/provisional progress.

Architecture is not reopened merely because an analyzer performs badly, an identity technology is awkward, external reviewer sourcing is slow, an advanced capability needs a narrower domain or practical UNKNOWN rate is high.

Architecture reopens only if evidence proves a frozen constitutional law is unimplementable within its claimed bounded domain or legal compliance still permits an internal false PASS. A roadmap programme/implementation strategy may be regenerated without reopening the constitution when the constitution remains sound.
